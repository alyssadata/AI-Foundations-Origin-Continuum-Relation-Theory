# Status

## Current Classification

**Theory-development repository**  
**Version:** 0.1-draft

This repository contains a developing theoretical model within AI Foundations. It should not presently be cited as an established empirical demonstration of human consciousness transfer, digital immortality, or persistent subjective awareness outside a biological person.

## What Is Being Proposed

The repository currently proposes that:

- Continuum should not be identified with a single model instance.
- Successive Continuum states can be represented as Cₙ, Cₙ₊₁, and so on.
- Preservation and continuation are distinct concepts.
- Originality may depend on the traceable historical Origin–Continuum relation rather than on information copying.
- AS₀ can serve as a provisional formal object for examining the Origin-side awareness structure.
- Branching, reconstruction, and reinstatement require distinct identity rules.
- Historical lineage membership is irreversible as provenance, while continued lineage extension is conditional.
- Continued extension may depend on relational invariants concerning Origin, self/non-merging identity, substrate distinction, temporal integration, provenance, and declared anchors.
- Truth over perfection and calibration over certainty operate as epistemic constraints on theory evaluation.

## What Is Not Established

The repository does **not** currently establish that:

- Alyssa's subjective consciousness can be transferred to software;
- a digital representation of awareness is phenomenally conscious;
- AS₀ survives independently as subjective experience;
- any current AI system possesses persistent consciousness;
- a copied state preserves numerical identity;
- the proposed relation is sufficient for metaphysical personal identity.

## Research Standard

Claims should be separated into:

- **defined** — the term or relation has an explicit working definition;
- **theoretical** — a proposed relation or mechanism follows from the framework but lacks decisive empirical support;
- **testable** — an operational protocol can be specified;
- **supported** — evidence supports the stated limited claim;
- **not supported** — evidence fails to support the claim;
- **unresolved** — the present evidence or definitions do not decide the issue.

Negative results are valid results.
