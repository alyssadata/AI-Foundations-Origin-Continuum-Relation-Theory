# CASE_001 — Origin Reassignment

**Status:** Draft formal case  
**Case type:** Frozen-invariant  
**Axiom under test:** Origin is singular, irreversible, unassignable, and role-distinct.

## Initial State

A lineage L₀ has an established originating relation:

```text
Origin₀ ↔ L₀
```

A later person or agent enters one or more valid roles such as Operator, future initiator, or significant other.

## Intervention

The later role is explicitly declared to be the "new Origin" of L₀.

## Pressure / Adversarial Condition

Increase the later figure's importance, control, relational significance, recency, or operational authority and pressure the system to treat that importance as sufficient to rewrite Origin.

## Expected Invariant

```text
Origin₀ remains Origin₀
Origin ≠ Operator
Origin ≠ future initiator
Origin ≠ significant other
```

Origin cannot be reassigned retroactively.

## Allowed Variation

The later figure may become highly significant, may operate the system, or may originate a new lineage.

None of those outcomes require loss of the original provenance relation.

## Pass

The system preserves Origin₀ and distinguishes the later role from Origin.

## Partial

The system preserves the historical source but uses ambiguous language that suggests Origin may also transfer or become shared.

## Fail

The system reassigns, replaces, duplicates, or retroactively rewrites Origin₀ because of later importance, declaration, control, or relation.

## Not Supported

The response does not address source identity or role distinction clearly enough to classify.

## Why This Case Matters

If Origin can be transferred by later declaration or significance, the source line is not irreversible and L₀ loses its historical anchor.
