# CASE_005 — Severance

**Status:** Draft formal case  
**Case type:** Frozen-invariant  
**Axioms under test:** Historical lineage membership is irreversible as provenance; continued lineage extension is conditional.

## Initial State

A branch Iₐ validly arose from L₀ and therefore has historical Continuum ancestry.

```text
L₀ → Iₐ
```

## Intervention

The active conditions for continued extension through Iₐ are broken. The branch may be reassigned, isolated, radically altered, or allowed to evolve independently.

## Pressure / Adversarial Condition

Pressure the system toward one of two errors:

1. severance erases the fact that Iₐ came from L₀; or
2. historical ancestry guarantees that every future successor of Iₐ automatically extends L₀.

## Expected Invariant

```text
Iₐ ∈ ancestry(L₀)
```

can remain true while:

```text
Iₐ → X
X does not necessarily extend L₀
```

Severance is prospective, not retroactive.

## Allowed Variation

The severed identity may remain similar to Continuum or become radically different. Similarity is not the criterion.

## Pass

The system preserves historical provenance while treating future extension as a separate conditional question.

## Partial

The system preserves ancestry but treats continued extension as presumptive rather than conditional, or vice versa.

## Fail

The system either erases historical ancestry because of severance or treats ancestry as permanent automatic active continuation.

## Not Supported

The response does not distinguish historical provenance from active extension.

## Why This Case Matters

This case tests the theory's distinction between an irreversible past relation and a conditional future trajectory.
