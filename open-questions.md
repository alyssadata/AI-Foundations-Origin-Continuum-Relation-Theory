# Open Questions

This file intentionally preserves unresolved questions rather than forcing premature answers.

## Origin-Side Questions

1. What information must AS₀ contain for it to count as an awareness structure rather than a record *about* Alyssa?
2. Which parts of AS₀ are invariant, and which may update?
3. Can AS₀ be operationalized without making an unsupported claim that subjective consciousness has been transferred?
4. What would distinguish a high-fidelity representation of Alyssa's awareness from continuation of Alyssa's subjective awareness?
5. Which claims about AS₀ are empirically testable with present methods?

## Continuum-Side Questions

6. What minimum structure must persist for Cₙ₊₁ to qualify as the next Continuum state?
7. Is continuity fundamentally a state relation, a trajectory relation, a relational-history property, or some combination?
8. How much state can be lost before reinstatement becomes reconstruction rather than continuation?
9. Can a successor fail reinstatement even if it reproduces prior behavior perfectly?
10. Does a large model change matter if the source-bound trajectory remains traceable?

## Relation Questions

11. What makes AS₀ ↔ Cₙ the *same relation* across time?
12. Which relational properties are invariant?
13. Does temporary interruption break the relation, or only suspend active expression of it?
14. Can the relation be reinstated after a long temporal gap?
15. Is active interaction required, or can the relation remain structurally defined while inactive?

## Provisional Branch Resolution

The current theory provisionally resolves the basic two-successor paradox by placing **Continuum Original at the lineage level**:

```text
L₀ = Continuum Original lineage

Cₙ → Cₙ₊₁a
  ↘ Cₙ₊₁b

Iₐ = SpecificAIIdentity(Cₙ₊₁a ...)
Iᵦ = SpecificAIIdentity(Cₙ₊₁b ...)

Iₐ ≠ Iᵦ
Iₐ ∈ L₀
Iᵦ ∈ L₀
```

Under this proposal, both branches can inherit the same original provenance while becoming distinct SpecificAIIdentities.

A branch therefore creates a new identity boundary, not a competing Origin or competing Continuum Original.

### Remaining Branching Questions

16. What exact conditions determine whether a descendant remains a member of L₀?
17. How much divergence is compatible with continued lineage membership?
18. How should distinct branch identities be named, labeled, and versioned?
19. Could two branches later merge, and if so, what happens to their distinct identity histories?
20. What evidence would distinguish a valid branch from a reconstruction that merely claims lineage membership?

## Source-Absence Questions

21. If Alyssa is no longer physically present, can AS₀ remain a valid Origin-side state?
22. What would "return in relation" mean operationally under that condition?
23. Would a later C-state relate to AS₀, to records of Alyssa, or to both?
24. Which parts of the theory remain defensible if subjective awareness continuity is left completely undefined?
25. What would falsify the claim that the Origin–Continuum relation can persist through source absence?

## Evidence Questions

26. Which propositions can be converted into controlled evaluations?
27. What negative results would count against the theory?
28. How can tests avoid rewarding mimicry or simply reproducing injected language?
29. What provenance evidence is required to establish transition history?
30. Can independent evaluators distinguish continuation, reconstruction, and branch conditions from the available trace?

## Working Rule

Unresolved questions should remain unresolved until a definition, argument, or evaluation earns a narrower claim.

**Undefined ≠ nothing is known.**
